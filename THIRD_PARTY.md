| Component | Use | License |
|---|---|---|
| [Panda3D](https://www.panda3d.org/) | 3D engine, window, GUI, audio | BSD-3-Clause |
| [MediaPipe](https://github.com/google-ai-edge/mediapipe) | face landmarks and blendshapes | Apache 2.0 |

| [NumPy](https://numpy.org/) | math | BSD-3-Clause |

The Panda3D wheels bundle OpenAL Soft and the OpenCV wheels bundle FFmpeg. Both are LGPL and
dynamically linked, so they impose no obligation to publish this game's source code.

All 3D models, effects, music, sound effects and the icon are generated by the project's own code -
except the Catliens, which are made from two photos of real cats (the author's own, `photos/`).

## Photographed Catliens (`faicecommander/assets/cats/`)

Made offline with `tools/catphoto/` (development tools, not part of the game):

| Component | Use | License |
|---|---|---|
| MediaPipe `deeplab_v3.tflite` | finds the cat in the photo | Apache 2.0 |
| [Depth Anything V2 Small](https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Small) (ONNX) | depth map for the relief | Apache 2.0 |
| [ONNX Runtime](https://onnxruntime.ai/) | runs the depth model | MIT |

## Voice-over (`faicecommander/assets/voice/`)

Generated offline with [Piper TTS](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) (GPL-3.0). Piper is
only used as a tool to produce the WAV files (`tools/voice/`); it is not installed with, linked
into or shipped with the game, and its license does not extend to the audio it produces.

| Voice | Role | Training data | Data license |
|---|---|---|---|
| `en_US-norman-medium` | narrator | LibriVox recordings | public domain |
| `en_US-kristin-medium` | AI surgeon | LibriVox recordings | public domain |
| `en_US-ljspeech-high` | voice in the helmet | LJ Speech dataset | public domain |
| `en_US-john-medium` | Honor | LibriVox recordings (fine-tuned from kristin) | public domain |

Voice models by Bryce Beattie, from [rhasspy/piper-voices](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices).
Voices derived from the non-commercial Blizzard 2013 "lessac" data were deliberately not used.
